⬡ QVAULT Wallet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 June 2026

QVAULT Wallet (the “App”) is a non-custodial companion app for the $QVLT token on the Solana blockchain. This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

1. We never access your keys

The App does not create, store, or have access to your private keys or seed phrase. All signing is performed by the third-party Phantom wallet on your device. The App only receives your public wallet address and a session token after you explicitly connect.

2. Information the App uses

  • Public wallet address — to display your balances and build transactions. Public addresses are already visible on the public blockchain.
  • On-device session data — the Phantom connection session is stored locally on your device only. It is never transmitted to us.

3. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, private keys, or any personally identifying information. The App has no accounts, no analytics SDKs, and no advertising trackers.

4. Third-party services

The App communicates with the following services to function:

  • Phantom — wallet connection and transaction signing.
  • Solana RPC nodes — to read balances and broadcast transactions you approve.
  • Price APIs (Jupiter, CoinGecko) — to display token prices. No wallet data is sent to them.

Each service has its own privacy policy.

5. Camera

The App requests camera access only when you choose to scan a QR code to send tokens. Camera images are processed on-device and never stored or transmitted.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.

8. Contact

Questions: reach us on X at @TheQVault or via qvlt.xyz.

QVAULT is a community project. $QVLT is a utility token, not an investment product. Never share your seed phrase — the team will never DM you first.
