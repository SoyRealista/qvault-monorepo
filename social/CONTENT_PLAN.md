# QVAULT — Social Content Plan (X / Twitter)

A simple, repeatable system so the account always has something on-brand to say.

## Voice
Confident, smart, a little contrarian. We make a complex threat (quantum) feel
understandable and urgent — without fear-mongering or empty hype. Community-first,
never "financial advice." Tasteful meme energy, credible substance.

## 5 content pillars (rotate them)
1. **🧠 Quantum education** — explain the threat in plain language; myth-busting; "did you know."
2. **💸 Product & utility** — staking tiers, fees, buyback & burn, governance, vesting.
3. **🧬 Community & culture** — memes, ⬡ rituals, shout-outs, "you're early" energy.
4. **📰 News-jacking** — react to real quantum / crypto-security headlines (this is our edge).
5. **🔍 Transparency** — roadmap progress, devnet milestones, "building in public," treasury/burn stats.

## Cadence (with the daily bot)
- **1 post/day** baseline via the queue (the bot drips it out).
- Aim for this weekly mix: 2 education · 1 product · 2 community · 1 news-jack · 1 transparency.
- **News-jacks are time-sensitive** → add them with a near-term `scheduledFor` so they jump the queue, or post manually.

## News-jack template (fill in fast when a headline drops)
> "Today's headline: <quantum/security news in 1 line>.
> This is exactly why QVAULT exists. We're not waiting for the break — we're
> building quantum-safe finance now. ⬡ $QVLT"

## Rules of the road
- Each tweet ≤ 280 chars. Threads for anything longer.
- ≤ 1–2 hashtags; lean on the ⬡ glyph and $QVLT cashtag as signature.
- No price predictions, no "guaranteed returns," no financial advice.
- Respect X automation rules: no aggressive auto-follow/auto-DM/spam.
- Mind the unresolved "QVAULT" trademark in public claims.

## How to add content
Append to `content/queue.json` (see README). Keep a healthy backlog (2+ weeks)
so the bot never runs dry.
